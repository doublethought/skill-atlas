## Short Description

Skill Atlas is an open source dashboard for mapping product design skills, role readiness, and team archetypes. Clone it, customize the competency model, and use it as a starting point for your own design team.

## LinkedIn Draft

I made Skill Atlas public: an open source product design skill mapping dashboard for managers and design leads.

It helps teams compare skill shapes, track role maturity and fit, and see balance across Craft, Systems, and Strategy archetypes. It also includes AI-Augmented Design as a first-class competency.

The repo is built to be cloned and customized: it runs locally with demo data, can use PostgreSQL for persistence, and includes setup notes plus screenshots.

## Website Blurb

Skill Atlas is a customizable product design skill mapping dashboard. It gives design managers a clear way to compare skills, role readiness, role fit, and team archetype balance across a product design team.
